## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the ethos of a high-end bespoke design and branding atelier, catering to refined wellness, pilates, physiotherapy, and boutique events. The visual atmosphere balances meticulous architectural rigor with welcoming, sunlit tactility.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Curated & Authorial:** Evoking deliberate craftsmanship, quiet confidence, and tailored luxury rather than sterile software utility.
- **Warm & Organic:** Soft ambient illumination, natural porcelain, warm linen, and terracotta nuances replace cold tech blues and harsh slates.
- **Frictionless & Trustworthy:** Precision micro-interactions, legible administrative forms, and cryptographic security cues create effortless client onboarding.

### Design Movement: Warm Glassmorphism
The aesthetic combines frosted glassmorphism with organic light modeling:
- Translucent white modules layered over a warm 3-stop linen-to-porcelain gradient.
- Dual-source ambient shadows: a brand terracotta radiance blended with low-contrast occlusion for floating depth.
- Glass edge highlights and precise hair-thin rims (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)` top light reflection) establishing physical sheet-of-glass metaphors.
- Vibrant, energetic terracotta orange used sparingly to command primary action, paired with dark slate contrast containers for executive grounding.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high luminance contrast, diffuse warmth, and distinct functional separation.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#F97316` / `#E65100`):** The signature terracotta tone. Used exclusively for primary calls to action, active navigation tabs, interactive selection rings, and progress markers. Never overused on secondary elements.
- **Secondary (`#1E293B`):** Deep charcoal slate. Reserved for executive profile cards, dark mode emphasis panels, and high-contrast anchors within the warm layout.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488`):** Cryptographic teal. Dedicated solely to security badges, encrypted states, PIN verification cues, and locked scope metadata.
- **Neutral (`#475569`):** Slate body color, tuned to maintain AA contrast across both translucent glass surfaces and warm gradients.
- **Focus Indigo (`#4F46E5`):** Strictly restricted to field focus outlines (`ring-2 ring-[#4F46E5]`). Never applied to buttons or surface fills.

### Semantic & Feedback Colors
- **Success (`#10B981`):** Completed steps, approved milestones, positive pipeline columns. Badges utilize 10% alpha fill with a darker shade for copy.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Impending deadlines, items in progress, or pending client actions.
- **Urgent (`#EF4444`):** SLA breaches, PIN errors, destructive operations, and stalled leads (>5 days).
- **Info (`#0284C7`):** Non-critical operational notifications and updates.

### Surfaces & Glass Overlays
- **Canvas Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #FBF8F3 0%, #F5EFEB 50%, #ECE4DB 100%)`.
- **Glass Sheet:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)` combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
- **Active Selection Surface:** Light warm tint `#FFF7ED` framed with a `#F97316` border.
- **Highlight Card (One per screen maximum):** Linear gradient from `#F97316` to `#FBBF24` with sharp white typography.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes modern geometric clarity and high legibility. Two fonts drive the entire experience:
1. **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Primary structural, editorial, and UI face. Its wide counters and geometric balance provide an inviting yet clean studio look.
2. **JetBrains Mono:** Specialized face strictly utilized for alphanumeric identifiers (`#PLT-2026-08`), security PINs (`842 910`), short URLs, and machine-generated codes.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Execution Rules
- **Display KPIs:** Rendered at 36px/700 with `-0.02em` tracking. Paired with uppercase category descriptors set at 12px/700 with `0.05em` letter spacing.
- **Screen Titles:** Crisp 28px/700 headers accompanied by 14px regular supporting text.
- **Body Text:** Standardized at 14px/400 with a 20px line height in `#475569`.
- **Hard Minimum:** Typography must never fall below 11px on any device.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an 8pt base grid with 4pt half-step subdivisions for micro-spacing.

### Desktop Spatial Architecture (1440px Canvas)
- **Left Dock:** A fixed, floating glass dock (72px wide) positioned with an outer offset of 20px from screen boundaries.
- **Primary Content View:** Padded by 32px (`2rem`) margin around canvas sections, with fluid interior columns.
- **Card Padding:** Fixed standard of 24px (`1.5rem`), providing breathing space around internal content.
- **Module Grid Gap:** 20px (`1.25rem`) uniform gap across Kanban columns, metric cards, and split views.

### Mobile Reflow Architecture (390px Viewport)
- The left floating dock is suppressed completely. Navigation moves to a compact bottom bar or top identity strip depending on role (studio vs. client).
- Canvas margins reduce to 20px (`1.25rem`) with gutter gaps tapering to 16px (`1rem`).
- Form elements scale horizontally to fill 100% of the content width.
- Synthetic browser frames and technical footers (build numbers, latency readouts) are prohibited across all viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through material layering rather than heavy opaque drop shadows. The design layers glass sheets above the warm diffuse background.

### The Glassmorphic Formula
- **Glass Module Fill:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)`.
- **Blur Filter:** `backdrop-filter: blur(20px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
- **Rim Light (Top & Border):** 1px solid stroke with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)` highlighting light coming from the top-left canvas source.
- **Dual Ambient Shadow:**
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(230, 81, 0, 0.07), 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04);`
  The orange tint gives elements an atelier glow, while the faint neutral occlusion grounds the floating surface.

### Interaction Elevation
- **Card Hover:** Subtle elevation lift via shadow expansion: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(230, 81, 0, 0.12), 0 12px 28px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.
- **Dragged State (Kanban cards):** Elevated scale (`scale(1.02)`), rotated by `1.5deg`, casting an expanded warm shadow (`rgba(230, 81, 0, 0.16)`).
- **Tactile Button Press:** Primary CTA contracts smoothly on click via `active:scale-[0.98]`.

## Shapes

The interface balances soft geometry with organic forms, matching the refined aesthetic of modern wellness and design brands.

### Corner Radius System
- **Floating Lateral Dock:** `24px` radius (`rounded-3xl`), creating a floating capsule silhouette.
- **Primary Cards & Modals:** `20px` radius (`rounded-2xl`) for structural containment.
- **Input Fields & Action Buttons:** `12px` radius (`rounded-xl`), providing a soft, tactile touch target.
- **Badges, Tags & Status Pills:** `9999px` (`rounded-full`) for high-contrast scanning.
- **Active Dock Icons:** `12px` rounded-square container hugging the icon.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary CTA:**
  - Height: `44px` (or `48px` on client form inputs).
  - Background: Linear gradient `135deg, #F97316 0%, #E65100 100%`.
  - Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans, 14px, weight 600, `#FFFFFF`.
  - Border Radius: `12px`.
  - Hover: Background shifts to `#EA580C`.
  - Active: `scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary CTA:**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
  - Text: `#0F172A`, weight 600.
  - Hover: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Icon Buttons:** `40px x 40px` frosted glass target with Material Symbols Outlined (20px stroke).

### 2. Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container:** Height `44px` (studio) or `48px` (client flows).
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`.
- **Border:** 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- **Text:** `#0F172A`, 14px (minimum 12px on helper tags).
- **Placeholder:** `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus Ring:** Strict Indigo outline: `ring-2 ring-[#4F46E5] ring-offset-1 border-[#4F46E5]`.
- **Error State:** Border `#EF4444`, red focus ring, and helper text `#EF4444`.

### 3. PIN Code Input
- Six separate box inputs or unified monospaced input.
- Font: JetBrains Mono, 24px, weight 600, tracking `0.1em`.
- Center aligned, auto-advancing, with light frosted surface.
- Error state: horizontal shake animation with red boundary.

### 4. Cards & Containers
- **Standard Glass Card:** 24px internal padding, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)` fill, 20px radius, top light border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)`.
- **Signature Highlight Card:** Only one per viewport. Gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #F97316 0%, #FBBF24 100%)`, solid white typography.
- **Executive Profile Card:** Solid or translucent dark fill `#1E293B`, subtle white text, dedicated to high-level studio credentials.

### 5. Badges & Chips
- Padding: 4px horizontal, 8px vertical; border radius `9999px`.
- Success: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.10)`, text `#047857`.
- Warning: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.10)`, text `#B45309`.
- Urgent: Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.10)`, text `#B91C1C`.
- PIN / Security: Background `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.10)`, text `#0F766E`.

### 6. Floating Left Dock (Studio Navigation)
- Width: `72px`, height: `calc(100vh - 40px)`, radius: `24px`.
- Frosted glass finish with top HD monogram mark.
- Icon Stack: Início, CRM, Forms Hub, Projetos, Agenda, Clientes, Configurações.
- Active Indicator: Orange square container (`#F97316`, `12px` radius) with solid white icon.
- Dock Footer: Clean theme-switch toggle and personal avatar.
